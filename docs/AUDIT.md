# Chapter audit — the work list for II–VIII

Working notes from a seven-agent audit of Chapters II–VIII against `docs/STYLE.md`.
Each chapter's full structured audit is regenerated by the wtfk-style-and-audit workflow; this is the summary
that drives the rework. Word counts are `node tools/prose-count.js`; fit numbers are `node tools/scan-fit.js`.

## The finding that matters most

Across all seven chapters, **almost no puzzle is genuinely four-handed**. The recurring failures are the same
three: a fact that is supposed to live on one phone is also printed on the Hearth or in the shared Book; two
roles are told the same thing; or the search space is small enough that free retries replace a role entirely.
The honourable exceptions, which should be preserved as models: `ch5_count`, `ch7_fourhands`, `ch8_code`.
